How it works

  1. Select a paragraph or passage in Word.
  2. Open FocusRead.
  3. Choose a strength.
  4. Click Apply FocusRead.
  5. Click Restore original when finished.

FocusRead is a reading aid. It does not diagnose or treat ADHD, dyslexia or any other condition.
